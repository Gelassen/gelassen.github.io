Executive summary

During past week I had designed a protective cascade for connecting the onboard computer to the drone.

The next version of my custom drone requires an onboard computer and it should be a part of the rest of electronics. Different parts of electronics which are not intended to work together by manufacturer requires additional parts to protect them from malfunctioning. This custom pcb solves exactly this problem.

This is a not a sophisticated pcb, but this is my first experience *designing* it to bridge the gap between projects needs and lack of market capability to fullfil this needs. That's why the production chain and technological process uncovered here may be of value to my potential clients.

The problem

The second version of my custom drone requires a video stream from the sky and some custom operations with it on the board. For this aim onboard computer should be installed. Upgrade to 21 - 38 TOPS class computer is planned to support full autonomous navigation.

The problem is chosen computer is intended to work with official power supply adapter. It has an option to power it over another source connected over GPIO pins. However, connecting the battery and computer directly would require aligning the voltage and current levels. Also we have to protect from different kind of threats. That's exactly what official power adapter does. Therefore we just need to implement out own for powering the computer from the battery.

Market research shown a decent DC-to-DC converter К480508 with supported voltage range to meet with all kind of batteries and required output 5V with up to 8A.

What the market lacks of is a protection circuite to protect the computer from overvoltage, misplaced polarity, backfeeding and shortage. That's where idea of custom made pcb comes from.

The solution

Design the principle electronic scheme

We have 5V5A input and 5V5A output. In the connection between them we have to add fuse to prevent overcurrent, diode to prevent backfeeding and misplaced polarity.

However diode is not a good option here. The current has some small voltage drop when passing through the diode. For 5V basic power supply it is a important. Therefore MOSFET transistor is used instead. Resistor on its gate regulates when it would be opened and fuse on the source protects the circuit from overcurrent. The principle of the scheme also prevents backfeedning.

Besides that such elements and dynamic work of the drone must introduce noise in the current which should be filtered and smoothed. For this aim LC filter over inductor and chain of capacitors is used. Capacitors has diminishing capacity size to filter out different frequencies of noise. The important aspect is to keep the distance between capacitors and inductor and capacitors and output as small as possible.

Design the PCB

Kicad has been used to design both the principle scheme and PCB. It allows automatic export of the scheme to PCB. It doesn't do this perfect, so extra steps are required.

The first one is to choose footprints. Each element of the scheme has its own form factor and its own pin configuration, it is important to find a right one among rich library offers.

The next one is wiring. All elements should be connected to each other. Two tricky things had been discovered here.

The first one is footprint pining. If the footprint has not been chosen right, pins would have a different positions, therefore you will get not correct wiring.

The second one is missed connection when the actual connection you have made already. You observe the line between two elements, but software claims there is no such connection. It might be important because all data behind would be transferred into different format for machine printing and it would operate with actual data rather than with visual view. A mistake here could lead to the wrong actual result. The solution for this issue was to properly name all segments of the connection line. Each segment between two elements of the scheme should has its own name.

The last part is the size of connection line and ground line. 5A is quite strong current, it requires thick lines near 1.5mm when general size is near 0.2 mm. In this scheme MOSFET pins do not allow such thick lines to connect to without shortage with another neighbor line. It had been solved by keeping short thin lines directly to pins and inserting them into thick line as soon as possible.

The ground zone for pcb like this is recommended to fill the whole pcb surface. Kicad allows to do this automatically emphasizing & separating from the GND connection lines and components.

Export in the gerber format for manufacturer

Manufacturer had asked me to prepare a gerber file. It seems like a standard format for machines that produces pcb. Kicad allows to do it too by choosing which layers to add to the final file.

Search for manufacturer & purchase

Market research shown several offers of custom pcb manufacturing, as well as a local and as a proxy to chinese main companies. In the process of negotiation I recognized the need to convert my principle scheme to PCB design and export it to gerber format. Meanwhile it was discovered geber has two formats -- legacy and an actual one. Kicad used by default the modern one, but manufacturer faced with difficulties to open it, so I had to fallback to a legacy one. Something worth to remember when dealing with producing vendor.

Expecting result

PCB project has been sent to the manufacturer to request his price and production time. After chosen the best offer the pcb should be finished. Later it will be verified with lab power supply to confirm my calculations and installed on the drone to confirm its work on the board in the sky.
